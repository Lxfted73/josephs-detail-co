What we need on site

This is mobile — we come to your driveway. We bring the hose and sprayer. You provide a working outdoor spigot and a live electrical outlet for the shop-vac and compressor. Shade is recommended, not required. Morning or late afternoon keeps UV from flashing Quik Wax on a hot panel.

$10 deposit

A $10 deposit holds interior, exterior, or the combo. It comes off the visit total on the day — $50 or $100 on a sedan, $60 a side or $120 combo if the vehicle is oversized. Mildew, stains, odor, or swirls can be present — we still only do the maintenance visit, and those problems stay. Cancel by the night before and the deposit is not kept. Same-day no-show keeps the $10.

  • Paint correction, ceramics, engine bays
  • Mold / mildew treatment
  • Deep smoke, rot, or set-in urine odor
