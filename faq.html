Mobile detailing

We come to you. Here is what that takes.

Hose and sprayer ride with us. A working spigot and a live outlet have to be at your place. Shade is recommended, not required — time of day matters more than a tree.

Do you come to my house?

Yes. Joseph's Detail Co. is mobile. Port Orchard and nearby Kitsap. You stay home; we set up in the driveway.

What do I need to have ready?

  • A working outdoor spigot we can hook our hose to
  • A live electrical outlet within cord reach for the shop-vac and compressor
  • Room to walk around the car with two buckets
  • Cabin emptied of trash, valuables, and loose gear

You bring the hose?

Yes — hose and sprayer ride in the vehicle. We still need your faucet. No water on site means we cannot do the exterior.

Why the outlet?

The shop-vac and the compressor run on household power. Compressor is set between 40 and 70 PSI — no rubber tip on the gun. A dead outdoor outlet or a cord that cannot reach the car means interior blow-out and drying get cut. Tell us before we drive over.

Is shade required?

No. It is recommended. Ultimate Quik Wax wants a panel that is cool and dry. Midday sun on dark paint will flash the product. Morning, late afternoon, or a cloudy PNW stretch is easier. If the only slot is high sun, we work panel-by-panel.

What if it rains?

Light mist: exterior can wait or we reschedule. Interior-only is fine under a carport or in a garage with an outlet. We will not Quik Wax in active rain — the film cannot set.

Can you get the stains / smell / swirl marks out?

No. We can still come if mildew, stains, odor, or swirls are present. We will not treat them. Installed carpet is vacuum only. Removable mats get washed and must be dry before they go back. No extraction, dye, mold treatment, odor work, paint correction, or ceramics. You leave with a maintained car that still has the original problem. The book form has a radio for that.

How does the $10 deposit work?

After photos confirm a maintenance job, $10 holds interior, exterior, or the combo and comes off the visit total. No deposit if we decline. Cancel by the night before and it is not kept. Same-day no-show keeps the $10. How to send it comes in the confirmation text.

How often should I book?

Two or three times a year for a daily driver that lives outside. This is maintenance, not a monthly package.

What do you actually put on the car?

Gold Class two-bucket wash, Hot Rims on cool wheels (soap only on unknown / polished / chrome), clay only if the baggie test is gritty, thin Quik Wax, leather cleaner then conditioner, Natural Shine on vinyl. No Windex, tire foam, Wash & Wax, or carpet soap. Product list is on Services.

Should I send pictures?

Yes — pictures of the spot that bothers you. We’ll look and say if it’s a maintenance job. Book.

How long are you here?

About one hour per side on a sedan or small crossover. About two hours for the $100 combo. A full-size truck, 3-row SUV, or van adds about 30–45 minutes, not a second visit. Clay still only happens if the baggie test is gritty.

Do trucks and big SUVs cost more?

Menu price is $50 a side and $100 together on a sedan or small crossover. Full-size trucks, 3-row SUVs, and vans add $10 on interior-only or exterior-only, and $20 on the combo. That is extra wash passes and vacuum path, not a different service. Mid-size SUVs and short-bed trucks stay at menu price. $10 deposit still comes off whatever the total is.

What do you do with my photos?

Intake photos quote the job. After photos may go in a completed-work gallery if you check yes on the book form — plates cropped, no kids, no house numbers. Not sold. Say no and they stay off the site. Delete request: email us.
